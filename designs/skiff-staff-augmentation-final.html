About staff augmentation

Between hiring and outsourcing, there's a better option.

Staff augmentation means senior engineers join your existing team directly — your stack, your tools, your process — instead of you waiting months to hire, or handing a whole project to an outside agency.

In-house hiring

Full ownership, slow to fill

Total control over who joins your team — but months of sourcing and interviewing before anyone writes a line of code.

Trade-off: time & fixed headcount
Project outsourcing

Hands-off, but out of your hands

Fast to start, but delivery runs on someone else's process — your visibility and day-to-day control drop.

Trade-off: control & context
